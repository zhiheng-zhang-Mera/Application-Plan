## Why SUTD moved into ACTIVE

SUTD is unusually clean against the current constraints:

- GRE: **not required**, only highly recommended.
- English: undergraduate English-medium study waives TOEFL/IELTS.
- Academic wording: bachelor's degree or above with **excellent academic standing**, but no published numeric/classification floor was found on the current PhD admissions page.
- Funding: competitive SUTD PhD Fellowship and President's Graduate Fellowship are open to all nationalities and cover applicable tuition up to four years. From 2026, the international-student monthly stipend is **S$3,100** for the standard PhD Fellowship and **S$4,300** for the PGF; teaching/lab duties apply for subsidised fees.
- Research shape: the current ISTD PhD-opportunities page contains multiple projects that are directly systems/empirical/tool-building oriented rather than proof-centric.
- Urgency: Jan 2027 applications close **2026-09-30**.

### Friction that still exists

SUTD is not zero-friction: application requires three academic referees and a 3,000-word Statement of Objectives; shortlisted candidates may be interviewed. During ISTD candidature there is an oral QE, oral PE, and thesis oral defence. These are recorded as friction, not hidden.
