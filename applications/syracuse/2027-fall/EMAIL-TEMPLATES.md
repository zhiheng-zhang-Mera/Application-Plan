## C. 收到录取或资助安排后：核实 funding package

触发条件：真实录取/资助通知已到达。收件人应为该通知中指定的联系人；这里不猜测个人地址。

Subject: CISE PhD Offer - Funding and Appointment Details

Dear [Funding or Graduate Program Contact],

Thank you for the offer of admission to the CISE Ph.D. program for Fall 2027.

Before making my enrollment decision, could you please clarify the funding package and appointment conditions?

1. What stipend and tuition support are included, and for which months and academic years?
2. Will the support be a fellowship, research assistantship, teaching assistantship, or a combination?
3. What service duties and time commitments apply during each funding period?
4. Which mandatory fees and health-insurance costs are covered?
5. Is summer support included or separately competitive, and what conditions govern renewal in later years?

If these details are provided in a separate award or appointment letter, I would appreciate receiving that document.

Thank you for your assistance.

Best regards,
Zhiheng Zhang
