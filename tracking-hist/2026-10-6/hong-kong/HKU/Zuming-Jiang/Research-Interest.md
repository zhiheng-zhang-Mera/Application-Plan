# Brief Research Interest

I am interested in reliability testing for long-running, agentic and distributed software systems. My recent projects repeatedly exposed a gap between **process survival** and **task correctness**: a service can continue running while state, evidence or recovery behavior is already inconsistent.

DS-Hns studies this problem in long-horizon repository work with restart recovery and explicit terminal-state evidence. Utopia/Digital City adds a real multi-device environment in which failures can span device routing, availability and service state. Its research-strengthening work has been used for controlled failure injection, recovery measurement, replay and ablation.

For PhD research, I would like to study how testing can systematically reveal false-success and silent-corruption modes in autonomous software infrastructure. One direction is to generate reusable “failure shapes” across storage, device, network and agent-control boundaries, then require the system to preserve enough provenance for an independent reviewer to reproduce the failure and verify the repair.

This would let me connect my current systems-building work with research in software/system testing, reliability and security while producing both practical infrastructure and empirical evidence.
