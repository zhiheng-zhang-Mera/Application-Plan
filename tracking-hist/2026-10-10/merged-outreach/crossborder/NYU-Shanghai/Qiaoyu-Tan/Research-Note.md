# Research Interest — Trustworthy Long-Horizon Agents

**Zhiheng Zhang · PhD 2027 research concept for Qiaoyu Tan**

### Problem

Tool-using agents can produce fluent completion narratives even when dependencies are broken or a user-authorized goal was not achieved. Interrupted long-horizon runs amplify this failure because the reconstructed agent state may be inconsistent with verified prior work.

### Possible contribution

Study goal-grounded acceptance contracts and evidence-aware replanning rules, integrating executed tool outcomes with truthful status reporting rather than treating language-based self-evaluation as proof.

### Evaluation

Use controlled multi-step coding and personal-system tasks with replayable failures, compare against baseline agents, and report incorrect completion rates, intervention burden, dependency breakage and task-level success.

### Scope

My evidence today is an engineering substrate rather than a claim of new general-purpose trustworthy-AI algorithms or any previously published peer-reviewed theory.
