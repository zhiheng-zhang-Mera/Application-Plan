# Research Problems for RISE Lab

**Zhiheng Zhang · PhD 2027 research concept for Chengpeng Wang**

### Problem 1 — State-aware program analysis for coding agents

A coding agent can pass a local check while invalidating repository-wide dependencies. I propose to combine repository-state observations with lightweight static/dynamic evidence to identify stale assumptions and violated invariants before an agent commits to a revised plan.

### Problem 2 — Evidence-preserving recovery and outcome validation

After interruption or a failed tool call, a resumed agent may repeat work, lose earlier acceptance criteria or falsely announce completion. I would evaluate recovery against executable repository outcomes, retained verification receipts and consistency with user-authorized goals.

### Methods and boundary

Use representative multi-file tasks, fault injection, reset/replay and comparison against fixed-plan or agent-only baselines. Report both successful and failed trajectories, and distinguish prototype runtime evidence from a general reliability result.
