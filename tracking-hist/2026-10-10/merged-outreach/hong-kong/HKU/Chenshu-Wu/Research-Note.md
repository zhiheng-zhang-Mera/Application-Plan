# PhD Research Proposal — Reliable AIoT Execution

**Zhiheng Zhang · PhD 2027 research concept for Chenshu Wu**

### Motivation

Wearable and mobile interfaces can initiate user-authorized tasks whose execution runs for minutes or hours elsewhere. Context uncertainty, link loss and resource changes may interrupt the work, while traditional offloading metrics alone do not establish that the original user outcome remains satisfied.

### Research question

How should an AIoT personal-agent runtime combine contextual signals, edge-node availability and authorization constraints to choose placement and recover from disruption without silently changing its goal? The research mechanism would be software-first, while wireless sensing/localization from the host lab could supply contextual inputs.

### Evaluation plan

Compare fixed routing and adaptive policies in multi-stage tasks using reproducible failure injection, network interruptions, context-quality ablations, and real endpoint verification. Measure correct task outcomes, false completion, time-to-recovery, resources and intervention burden.

### Evidence boundaries

The current frozen Utopia work verifies a bounded two-Windows-worker and Android-control configuration. Dedicated wearable sensing, real-world longitudinal usability and full redundant-server failover remain proposed doctoral work rather than completed results.
