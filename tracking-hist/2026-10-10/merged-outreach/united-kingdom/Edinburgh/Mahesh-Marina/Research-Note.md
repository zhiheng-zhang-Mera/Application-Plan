# PhD Concept — Reliable Software-Centric Edge AI

**Zhiheng Zhang · PhD 2027 research concept for Mahesh Marina**

### Research premise

Mobile edge infrastructure must maintain more than throughput: a user-initiated AI workflow may depend on multiple stages, changing connectivity and long-lived state. Speeding up offloading is of limited use if the agent silently changes the goal or cannot substantiate its outcome.

### Systems hypothesis

A runtime that retains explicit goal contracts, execution receipts and bounded replanning decisions can reduce false-completion events during node/link disruption compared with fixed workflows and retry-only systems.

### Method

Prototype over existing Android/Windows endpoints and, where available, the lab's software-centric networking testbeds. Stress with failure injection and network changes; compare outcomes, restoration of state, user intervention and resource cost; separate physical evidence from simulation.

### PhD fit

This complements research in software-centric mobile networking and edge AI infrastructure. The systems mechanisms and evaluation would be adapted to the supervisor's projects rather than requiring adoption of Utopia itself.
