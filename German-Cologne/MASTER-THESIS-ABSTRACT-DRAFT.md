# Master's thesis abstract — draft for applicant verification

Author: Zhiheng Zhang
Program: Master of Computer Science, University of Melbourne
Supervisor: Professor Richard Sinnott
Working descriptive title: Evidence Traceability for Android Privacy Analysis
Status: Research in progress; official thesis title and final abstract require confirmation.

Android privacy analysis requires a careful distinction between observed behaviour and interpretations that the available evidence cannot establish. This master's research concerns Privacy Lens, an offline Android privacy-evidence prototype and a curated reproducibility artifact. The work organizes evidence admission, metric checks, and claim-to-evidence traceability to make the basis of privacy-related observations inspectable. The prototype uses a TypeScript/React Native/Expo application with a Kotlin native layer. Its research emphasis is on documenting what the collected evidence supports, preserving the boundary between observations and interpretations that require human review, and supporting reproducible inspection of the artifact. Final evaluation results and thesis conclusions remain pending and are not claimed in this draft.
